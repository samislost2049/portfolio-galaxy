Trade Governance

Resources & Compliance

Reference notes for shipping documentation, agricultural certification, material sourcing, customs clearance and data handling across KASA Traders shipments.

Shipping

Maritime & Bill of Lading Terms

Full-container and less-than-container shipments move through Jebel Ali and Mundra. Bills of lading are issued against confirmed booking, with original, telex-release or sea-waybill presentation agreed per shipment.

Agriculture

Phytosanitary & Fumigation

Agricultural consignments are inspected and certified by the relevant plant quarantine authority before export. Treatment records and inspection certificates travel with the commercial documents.

Fumigation & Silo Reports

Fumigation certificates and silo intake reports are available for commodity lots on request.

Furniture

Timber Legality & Sourcing

Teak and hardwood sourcing follows destination-market legality requirements. Species, origin and chain-of-custody declarations are matched to each production batch.

Logistics

Packaging & Crating Protocols

Export packing uses ISPM 15 heat-treated timber where required, with moisture barriers and reinforced crating for sea freight and project cargo.

Textiles

Silk Mark Certification

Textile consignments can be accompanied by Silk Mark certification where applicable, together with fibre-composition and care-labelling documentation.

Customs

Customs Clearance Schedules

Clearance schedules are coordinated with licensed brokers at Jebel Ali and Mundra. HS classification and duty treatment are confirmed for each shipment before dispatch.

Trade Policy

CEPA Agreement Terms

Eligible India-UAE shipments may claim preferential tariff treatment under the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement with a valid certificate of origin.

Banking

Letter of Credit (LC) Guidelines

Letters of credit are accepted on mutually agreed terms. Documents are presented through nominated banks and checked against the LC schedule before shipment.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Contact details submitted through inquiry forms are used only to respond to trade requests, prepare quotations and maintain shipment records. We do not sell personal information.

These notes are a convenience summary. The signed contract, letter of credit and shipment-specific certificates govern each transaction.
