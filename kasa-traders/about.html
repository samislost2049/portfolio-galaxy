The Four Pillars of KASA Governance

Every consignor and consignee transacting through our desks is protected by our stringent operational tenets.

01

Origin Provenance

Every textile bolt, teak log, and spice bag is inspected directly at farm, forest, or mill before container loading.

02

Phytosanitary Rigor

Pre-export fumigation, laboratory moisture testing, and global standard HACCP/ISO certifications across all agricultural shipments.

03

CEPA Tariff Optimization

Maximizing zero-duty and reduced-tariff benefits under the bilateral UAE-India economic pact for our buyers.

04

Ethical Craftsmanship

Fair compensation for generational handloom weavers in Varanasi, woodcarvers in Rajasthan, and spice harvesters in Kerala.
